Privacy Policy

album-sync · Last updated 2026-11-08

Google user data

  • Access: album-sync requests only two Google Photos permissions: photoslibrary.appendonly (upload items and create albums) and photoslibrary.readonly.appcreateddata (list the albums and items the app itself created, to avoid duplicates).
  • Use: the data is used only to upload the owner's own local photos and videos into the owner's own Google Photos library.
  • Storage: the OAuth token is kept only in the macOS Keychain of the owner's own computer. Album IDs and upload status are kept in a local file on that computer. Nothing is stored on any server operated by the app.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to, or sent to any third party. The app talks only to Google's own APIs.
  • Deletion: the app never deletes anything from Google Photos. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; removing the app from the computer removes all local data.

album-sync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
